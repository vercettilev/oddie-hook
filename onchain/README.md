# onchain — Oddie's own pari-mutuel program

**Status: written, built and tested. Not deployed, not wired to the app.**

## Why this exists

A program of this shape already runs on devnet at
`Ao8tBMVCUMMYAm8cbq2qehP78RCK5xNAPyaXv9TH5FZT`, but its **source never landed in
this repo**. That is not a filing problem, it is a product blocker:

- its `Market` account has no `creator` field, so there is nowhere to record who
  the 3% is owed to
- it has no fee instruction, so there is no way to pay it

Which is why `src/store/economy.ts` has to describe `CREATOR_FEE_BPS_REAL` as a
*proposed* rate, "logged as an intended fee" and never actually charged. The
creator fee is the entire reason a stranger bothers to tag a market into
existence, so a program that cannot pay it cannot carry this product's
economics. This one is ours and it can.

## The one economic difference from the play economy

Off chain, `creatorFeePlay` is an **additive grant**: the house funds it and it
structurally cannot make anyone's payout smaller.

On chain there is no house. The vault holds exactly what was staked, so the fee
comes **out of the pool** and winners are paid from what is left. Anyone porting
the off-chain copy to a real-money market has to change the words as well as the
numbers: here the fee is a deduction, and the UI must say so.

## Instructions

| | |
|---|---|
| `create_market` | opens the market and records `creator` + `creator_fee_bps` |
| `take_position` | stakes on a side; same-side stakes accumulate, side switching is refused |
| `resolve_market` | settles and **fixes the fee once**, so later claims cannot drift |
| `claim_winnings` | pari-mutuel share of pool-minus-fee, or a refund if nobody won |
| `claim_creator_fee` | the 3%, **pulled** by the creator signing for themselves |

### Why the fee is pulled, not pushed

Pushing at resolve would make settlement for everyone else depend on the creator
having a funded account to receive into, and that person is usually someone who
tagged an argument on X and has never opened a wallet. A claim they sign keeps
settlement independent of them, and proves wallet ownership at the moment of
payment. A creator who never connects simply never claims; the fee stays in the
vault rather than being redirected to the house.

## What the tests pin

`tests/oddie_chain.ts`, 7 blocks, all passing against a local validator. Not
shape tests: every one pins a way the program could take or lose real money.

- the fee comes out of the pool, and payouts + fee can never exceed what was staked
- nobody claims twice, and nobody claims what is not theirs (not the loser, not
  a stranger, not the creator's fee)
- a market nobody won refunds instead of confiscating, and takes no fee, because
  charging someone to be handed their own money back is not a fee
- the vault is never drained below rent exemption, which would delete it and
  strand every unclaimed position

```bash
solana-test-validator --ledger /tmp/oddie-ledger --reset --quiet &
solana config set -u localhost && solana airdrop 100
anchor build
solana program deploy target/deploy/oddie_chain.so -u localhost
ANCHOR_PROVIDER_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8899 ANCHOR_WALLET=~/.config/solana/id.json \
  npx ts-mocha -p ./tsconfig.json -t 1000000 tests/**/*.ts
```

`anchor test` alone does not work here: anchor-cli 1.x reaches for `surfpool`,
which is not installed. The steps above use `solana-test-validator` instead.

## What putting a market on chain does and does not prove

`resolve_market` is signed by the market's authority. **A human still decides**
whether the FA left FIFA. On chain settlement makes it provable that *we said
so*, and provable what everyone staked and was paid. It does not make the
resolution itself trustless. Closing that gap is an oracle problem, and the
honest first step is committing `resolutionCriteria` on chain alongside the
question so the rule is fixed before the outcome is known.

## Before this can go live

1. A program keypair that is not the one in `target/deploy` (that one is
   generated by the build and is not a secret).
2. `declare_id!` and `Anchor.toml` set to the deployed address.
3. `src/chain/oddieChain.ts` repointed, and `create_market` given its two new
   arguments (`creator`, `creator_fee_bps`).
4. The real-money copy in the app changed from "proposed, not yet enforced" to a
   deduction that is actually taken.
5. A decision about custody and jurisdiction, which is a business question
   rather than a code one.
