How Oddie settles

A call in a stream chat settles off the stream itself. Oddie reads the result on screen, and the picture it read is public under the call.

A call settles off the stream

While a call is open, Oddie looks at the stream every few seconds. It settles the call only when it reads the same result twice in a row, a couple of seconds apart, and is sure both times.

It waits a minute after a call opens before it looks, so the end of the last game never settles the next call.

A call about a whole tournament or a night settles on the event’s own result screen, and a second, stronger model has to read it the same way.

When Oddie is not sure

The call stays open, and the streamer or a mod settles it:

!call yes
!call no

If the stream ends before a result, the call is canceled and nobody scores.

Every result can be checked

The picture Oddie settled a call on is kept, and it is shown under the call on the channel’s page on oddie.

Markets settle from their own rule

A market with SOL in it never settles from a picture. It settles from the rule written when it opened: a price claim from published price candles, anything else from public sources. Its rule, fee and refunds are on its own page.
