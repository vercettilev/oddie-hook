Privacy

Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version. We keep your public X profile, a random id for your browser, and your wallet address. We do not sell any of it, we do not run ads, and we never post as you.

What we keep, and why

  • Your X profile. When you connect X, we read what your profile already shows the world: your handle, display name, bio, join date, post count, follower counts, and your pinned post. We use it to build your Genesis card and to know which handle a ticket belongs to.
  • A device id. A random string stored in your own browser so that coming back to oddie finds your account without asking you to connect again. It is not tied to anything outside oddie.
  • Your wallet address. Public by nature. We use it to show your positions and to make sure one each taker counts once.
  • The markets you open and the bets you place. These are on a public blockchain, so they are public whether or not we keep a copy.

What we never do

  • We never post, reply, follow, or send a DM as you. Connecting X does not give oddie the ability to write from your account.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not run advertising.
  • We do not read your private messages, and we do not see anything on X that a logged-out visitor cannot already see.

Who else sees it

  • X handles the sign-in itself. Their terms and privacy policy apply to that step.
  • Solana. Every market, bet, payout and refund is written to a public blockchain. Anyone can read it, and nobody, including us, can delete it.
  • Our hosting and database provider, which stores the data above so the site can work.

That is the whole list. There is no analytics vendor, no ad network, and no data broker.

Cookies

oddie sets what it needs to work and nothing else: your device id, your sign-in session, and, for the operator, a preview cookie. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Getting your data out, or deleting it

The Disconnect button on the Genesis page unlinks your X account from this browser. To have your stored profile deleted, email lev@oddie.fun from the account you want removed, or send it from the X handle itself, and we will remove it.

What is already on chain cannot be deleted by us or by anyone. That is how a public blockchain works, and it is worth knowing before you take a side.

Children

oddie is not for anyone under 18.
