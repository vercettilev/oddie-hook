Read this part first. oddie runs on Solana mainnet. The SOL you stake is real money, it leaves your wallet when you take a side, and you can lose all of it. Take a side with money you are willing to lose.

What oddie is

You tag @oddiefun on a claim on X. A prediction market opens on that claim, on Solana, in its own vault. Anyone can take the YES side or the NO side. When the claim turns out true or false, the pool pays out.

Your wallet

You sign every transaction yourself. oddie never holds your private key and cannot move your funds. Once you take a side, your stake sits in that market's on-chain vault until the market settles or is refunded.

Opening a position costs a small amount of network rent, which Solana keeps. That is a cost of using the chain, not a fee oddie charges, and it does not come back to you.

Fees

A market charges 4% of the pool, once, at settlement. Half of it (2%) goes to whoever opened the market. Half (2%) goes to oddie. Both rates are written into the market when it is created, so a later change to our rates can never reprice a market that is already open.

There is no fee for placing a bet, and no fee if you lose. The 4% is taken from the pool before winners split it.

How a market is decided

The rule that decides a market is published when the market opens, before anyone takes a side. It does not change afterwards.

Settlement is done by oddie against the public record. A claim needs a date or a number, because a claim nobody can check is a claim nobody can settle. If the evidence is not there yet, the market waits rather than guessing.

If a market is never settled

Thirty days after a market closes, if it has not been settled, anyone can trigger a full refund of that market on chain. It does not need a signature from oddie, and we cannot block it. Your stake comes back to your wallet.

Genesis tickets

During Genesis, connecting an X account gives you 5 tickets. One ticket opens one market. A spent ticket comes back with every new taker, up to the same cap of 5.

Tickets are not currency. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and can be withdrawn if an account is used to spam. Your own wallet is never a taker.

Takers

A taker is a person whose first real-money bet on Oddie lands in a market you opened. Each person counts once, ever, however many of your markets they go on to bet in, and your own wallet is never a taker. The board ranks openers by how many takers they have.

A taker count is a number of people, not a balance. It is not currency, has no cash value, cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and is not a claim on any token, distribution or future payment. The only thing opening a market pays is the 2% creator fee described above, in SOL, at settlement.

What this is not

Nothing on oddie is financial, investment, or legal advice. Prediction markets can go to zero. Only take a side with money you are willing to lose.

You are responsible for whether you are allowed to use a product like this where you live, and for being old enough to. If you are not, do not use it.

Things that will get an account cut off

  • Opening markets to spam, farm tickets, or flood the queue.
  • Using more than one account, or somebody else's, to count as your own takers.
  • Claims targeting a private person, or content that is illegal where we operate.
